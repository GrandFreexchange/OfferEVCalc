Understanding Expected Value (EV)

In betting, as in markets, every decision has an Expected Value (EV). A profitable offer is simply one with a positive EV (+EV). This is calculated by finding the value of the bonus and subtracting the cost of acquiring it (the "cost of wagering"). We aim to find offers where the bonus significantly outweighs the cost.

Typical +EV Target

+£20.50 EV

(e.g., £100 Bonus @ 30x Wagering, 97% RTP Slot)

The Cost: Wagering Requirement (WR)

The Wagering Requirement is the primary cost. It dictates how much you must bet to unlock the bonus. The cost is the total wager multiplied by the game's house edge (100% - RTP). As WR increases, the offer's EV collapses.

Key Takeaway: EV turns negative around the 40x-50x WR mark for most bonuses. Low WR (sub-30x) is the primary signal of a profitable offer.

Profit vs. Cost Breakdown

This example shows a £50 bonus with a 25x WR on a 96% RTP slot. The total wagering cost (£50 * 25 * 4%) is £50. This is a break-even, £0 EV offer. Any lower WR or higher RTP makes it profitable.

Key Takeaway: Visualizing the EV as a "profit margin" helps quickly identify how much "edge" an offer has.

The "Free Spin" Fallacy

Free Spin (FS) offers are the most deceptive. Their value is not the number of spins, but the EV of their *winnings*. Offers with "0x Wager" (keep what you win) are highly profitable. Offers with high WR (e.g., 35x) on winnings often have a negative EV, as the cost to wager the winnings exceeds the winnings themselves.

Key Takeaway: Always hunt for 0x or low-wager (sub-10x) free spins. 100 spins at 0x WR is vastly superior to 300 spins at 50x WR.

Analysis & Process

Offer Analysis Process

1. Identify Bonus Value

£ Bonus OR (Spins x Spin Value)

▼

2. Find Wagering Requirement (WR)

Is it a multiplier (x) or currency (£)? Is it on (B) or (B+D)?

▼

3. Calculate Total Wager (TW)

e.g., (B+D) * WR (multiplier) OR Fixed £ Amount (currency)

▼

4. Determine Wagering Cost (WC)

TW * (100% - RTP). Use ~96-97% RTP for slots.

▼

EV = Bonus Value - Wagering Cost

(If > 0, it's a +EV offer)

Profitability Checklist

Use this checklist to find the key data points in the T&Cs.

  • ✓ Wagering Requirement: Low (0x-30x or low £)
  • ✓ Wagering On: Bonus only (if multiplier)
  • ✓ Eligible Games: High RTP (96%+)
  • ✓ Game Contribution: 100%
  • ✓ Max Win Cap: None or High
  • ✓ Max Bet: High (e.g., £5)
  • ✓ Time Limit: Reasonable (7+ days)
